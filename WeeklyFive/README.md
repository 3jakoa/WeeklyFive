# Weekly Five

A minimal iOS to-do app for people who hate complex systems.

Pick up to **5 priorities per week**.  
Everything resets on Monday.  
Start fresh.

---

## Why

Most to-do apps turn into backlogs.
Weekly Five is intentionally limited.

If it’s not one of your five priorities this week, it can wait.

No backlog.  
No guilt.  
No clutter.

---

## How it works

- Add up to **5 priorities** for the current week
- Mark items as done as you complete them
- When all 5 are done, the week is complete
- Every Monday, the list **resets automatically**

---

## Notifications

The app schedules a local notification every Monday morning
to remind you to create a new list for the week.

Notifications are optional and can be disabled in system settings.

---

## Data & privacy

- All data is stored **locally on the device**
- No accounts
- No cloud
- No tracking

---

## Built with

- SwiftUI
- Local notifications
- Local persistence
- ISO week-based reset logic
